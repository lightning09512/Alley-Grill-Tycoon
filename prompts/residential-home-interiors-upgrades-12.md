# Prompt pack: 12 playable residential home interiors

Generate each level as its own full-resolution portrait game-map image. Do not make a collage or show the outside of the house.

## Shared prompt

> Create exactly one standalone portrait 9:16 mobile-game interior map, ideally 1080×1920. Match the project's kitchen-map camera and art treatment: near-overhead 2D game-map view with slight 2.5D depth, hand-painted warm surfaces, bold readable outlines, practical Vietnamese home details, stable camera angle and tile scale.
>
> Show only the inside floor and furnishings, as if the camera is already inside the house looking down into the playable area. Let the floor continue to all image edges. Do not show a front entrance door, outside/street, façade, enclosing perimeter walls, visible building corners, roof, or a cutaway house shell. Avoid a real-estate perspective or an architectural floor-plan drawing. Short straight interior dividers may mark private zones, but leave wide open passages and do not form a closed maze. Keep the view focused on the interior map like the kitchen reference.
>
> This must read as one playable game map, with a continuous open floor and clear paths at least one character-width wide. The player walks in from off-screen at the bottom-center edge, so leave that edge open and connect its path to every room and interactive object. From level 5 onward, leave a second open, walkable continuation at the upper-center edge for future room maps; do not frame it with a door or outer wall. Keep stairs visible from level 6 onward at the back/right side; their upper floor is a separate future map, not stacked into this image. Place furniture so it never blocks paths, room transitions, stairs, or interaction points.
>
> Match the exterior home's realistic footprint: levels 1–5 are compact one-storey homes; levels 6–10 are narrow two-storey townhouses; levels 11–12 are broader two-storey villas. Show only the current floor. Interiors should become cleaner, more modern, larger and better equipped as the level rises. Preserve a grounded Vietnamese family-home feel, not a palace. No people, text, labels, UI, borders, collage, exterior views, enclosing walls/corners, or visible entrance doors.

## Level briefs

Use exactly one brief together with the shared prompt.

### Level 01 — Improvised shelter

> Tiny single-room ground floor, barely enough space for sleeping, cooking and washing. Patched tarp and scrap wood are visible as inner surface details only; stained cement floor, thin mat, plastic stool, bucket, one weak bare bulb, improvised single-burner stove, and a crude toilet in the same undivided corner, screened only by a faded cloth. Deliberately cramped, neglected and very low quality but non-graphic. No PC or TV. Preserve an open bottom-center path.

### Level 02 — Patched wooden hut

> Very small one-storey interior with patched timber surfaces, worn cement floor, low bed, plastic tubs, old fan and battered counter with portable burner. Add a toilet nook screened by a faded curtain; cooking and toilet are no longer touching but the space is still cramped and mismatched. No PC or TV.

### Level 03 — Old tin-roof home

> Narrow one-storey interior with a combined sleeping/living area, tiny kitchenette, and a separate toilet nook with wash bucket. Faded inner plaster, exposed-block patches, chipped concrete, second-hand table and basic gas stove. Still poor and dated, but more organized and cleaner than levels 1–2. No stairs, PC or large TV.

### Level 04 — Small basic home

> Modest one-storey interior with distinct living/sleeping, compact kitchen and small bathroom zones, all openly connected. Repaired plaster surfaces, inexpensive terracotta or plain ceramic flooring, narrow wooden table, simple bed, basic sink and gas stove, small barred window only if it does not create a visible exterior view. Sparse low-cost furniture. A tiny old CRT television may sit against the far edge; no PC.

### Level 05 — Tidy starter home

> Three compact connected zones on one floor: living/dining, a small bedroom and a proper bathroom, plus kitchenette along the living-room edge. Clean but inexpensive tiles, fresh pale inner surfaces, simple cabinets, folding dining table, standing fan and modest bed. Add a small flat-screen TV and an old shared laptop on the table. Open up a walkable continuation at the upper-center edge for future map expansion.

### Level 06 — First two-storey townhouse

> Ground-floor interior of a narrow two-storey townhouse: living zone near the bottom, compact kitchen/dining behind it and a small bathroom near the back. Add the first visible staircase/landing along the back/right edge; stairs lead out of frame to a separate future upper-floor map. Better tiles, repaired inner surfaces, proper kitchen counter, small fridge, modest sofa, older desktop PC and simple flat-screen TV. Preserve the open upper-center continuation.

### Level 07 — Improved tube house

> Cleaner, slightly better furnished narrow townhouse ground floor with distinct living, enclosed-feeling but openly accessible kitchenette, dining nook and bathroom. Oak-look cabinets, stainless sink, extractor hood, practical storage and compact sofa. Add a basic one-monitor desktop setup in a study nook and a modest flat-panel TV. Keep the fixed back/right stair location and open routes.

### Level 08 — Comfortable family townhouse

> Well-kept ground floor with connected living, dining and kitchen areas, separate bathroom and a small guest-room zone. Durable polished tile, fresh warm neutral inner surfaces, L-shaped cabinets, proper fridge, rice cooker, tidy dining set and extra storage. Add a mid-range desktop PC and a noticeably larger newer TV. Clearly more spacious and comfortable than level 7, but still a narrow townhouse, not a villa.

### Level 09 — Renovated city home

> Renovated narrow townhouse ground floor with living, dining nook, compact modern kitchen, small bathroom and a dedicated study room. Use short interior partitions/open transitions, never a closed maze. Large-format tile, warm off-white surfaces, matte cabinets, stone-look counter, built-in cooktop and extractor, comfortable sofa, good flat-screen TV and quality one-monitor PC. Keep stairs at back/right and clear paths.

### Level 10 — Modern townhouse

> Polished ground floor of a realistic narrow two-storey urban townhouse: living room, dining area, efficient kitchen, guest room, bathroom and compact office with all zones openly accessible. Light oak cabinetry, pale stone counter, integrated appliances, built-in storage and warm recessed lights. Add a larger modern TV and a capable two-monitor gaming/work PC. The whole interior is modern and premium while retaining the townhouse footprint.

### Level 11 — Upscale urban villa

> Wider ground-floor interior matching a two-storey urban villa: separate living, dining, kitchen, guest bedroom, bathroom and dedicated office, connected by generous open routes. Restrained premium wood, cream stone-look tile, quartz counters, integrated appliances, large high-quality TV and polished dual-monitor workstation. Affluent and comfortable, with a few plants, never palace-like. Keep the back/right stair visible.

### Level 12 — Contemporary villa

> Largest believable two-storey villa ground floor: open living and dining, full modern kitchen, guest bedroom, bathroom, office/gaming room and compact storage/laundry zone. Clearly wider and more spacious than level 11, with multiple open continuations at the upper/back edge for future room maps. Refined natural oak, pale stone, concealed storage, premium integrated appliances, warm indirect lighting, large premium TV and high-end multi-monitor PC. Preserve broad connected paths and visible back/right stairs. No extra storey, palace ornament or exterior shell.
